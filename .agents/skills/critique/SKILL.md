# Experience Critique

## Scope

Review a candidate or evidence package without editing its source or acceptance standard.

## Procedure

Judge hierarchy, cognitive load, visible cause and effect, pressure attribution, mobile readability, replay affordance, and expression originality against the frozen contract. Separate observations, inferences, suggestions, and blockers. A nearby screenshot, fixture trace, or sibling state does not cover the named branch.

## Acceptance

Return PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED, or NOT_RUN with exact evidence paths and the smallest actionable issue owner.
