# Competitor Research

## Scope

Study transferable player pressure and recovery patterns while preserving an original game expression.

## Procedure

For every reference, record what creates pressure, how it is signaled, how it escalates, how failure is attributable, and how recovery or replay works. Label each item as observed, inferred, or unknown. Convert the evidence into adopt, adapt, or reject implications and an explicit no-copy boundary. Keep source URLs, retrieval time, and content hashes with the structured record.

## Acceptance

Return only the structured research artifact. Never copy code, assets, names, UI, dialogue, content, or raw balancing values.
